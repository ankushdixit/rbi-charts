Key Insights

Bigger than Visa and Mastercard combined

In February 2026, UPI handled 20.4 billion transactions — more than Visa and Mastercard process globally in a month. India now accounts for 46% of all real-time payment transactions worldwide.

Growth is slowing — but still massive

Year-on-year growth decelerated from 97% (Feb 2022) to 33% (Feb 2025). This is natural at scale — but 33% growth on a $320B monthly system is still extraordinary.

COVID barely dented it

When India locked down in April 2020, cheques crashed 75%, credit cards fell 53%. UPI? Down just 20% — and recovered within a month. The lockdown actually accelerated adoption.

Average transaction value is falling

Average UPI transaction was ₹1,657 in Jan 2020. By Jan 2026, it's ₹1,306 — a 21% decline. UPI is penetrating deeper into small, everyday purchases: chai, auto rides, vegetable vendors.
